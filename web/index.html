Show one hand to your camera and hold a letter for about a second to type it.

Video stays on your device. Hand tracking and recognition run in this page.

Keyboard: 1–3 pick a suggestion, Space, Backspace, L and P switch modes.

Sign reference

Each drawing is a real hand pose from the training data, as tracked by MediaPipe (shown as the signer sees their own hand). J and Z involve motion; only their final position is shown and recognised.
